# 🚀 OmniStock
### Never Run Out. Never Overstock.

**An AI-powered predictive inventory management system designed to make commercial kitchens smarter, more efficient and data-driven.**

## 📌 About OmniStock

OmniStock is an AI-powered inventory intelligence platform designed to help restaurants, cafés, hostel messes, college canteens, corporate cafeterias and cloud kitchens manage their inventory more efficiently.

Traditional inventory management often relies on manual registers and static spreadsheets, making it difficult to predict when essential ingredients will run out or when excess stock will lead to unnecessary waste.

OmniStock aims to transform inventory management from simple stock tracking into predictive decision-making.

Instead of just telling businesses what is available today, OmniStock aims to answer:

**"When will we run out of stock, and how can we prevent it?"**

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Commercial kitchens frequently face three major inventory challenges:

- **Stockouts:** Essential ingredients run out during peak operating hours.
- **Overstocking:** Excess purchasing increases storage costs and wastage.
- **Manual Management:** Traditional inventory records make real-time monitoring and forecasting difficult.

These challenges affect operational efficiency, profitability and customer satisfaction.

## 💡 Our Solution

OmniStock introduces an intelligent inventory management approach that combines real-time stock monitoring, consumption analytics and predictive inventory insights.

The platform aims to help kitchen operators make smarter purchasing decisions, reduce preventable food waste and maintain uninterrupted operations.

## ⚙️ Key Features

- **Smart Inventory Tracking:** Monitor stock movements and inventory levels.
- **QR-Based Stock Management:** Simplify stock-in and stock-out operations.
- **Consumption Analytics:** Understand ingredient usage and consumption patterns.
- **Predictive Low-Stock Alerts:** Identify potential stock shortages before they occur.
- **Demand Intelligence:** Use menu and footfall information to improve inventory planning.
- **Multi-Outlet Management:** Support inventory visibility across multiple kitchen locations.
- **Purchase Planning:** Help businesses make informed replenishment decisions.

*Note: Features describe the product vision; availability depends on the current implementation.*

Potential revenue streams include:

- Monthly and annual subscriptions.
- Multi-outlet management plans.
- Enterprise and institutional packages.
- Future POS integration partnerships.
